QuoteKit: quotations and invoices for small businesses

Create a quotation, send it, turn it into an invoice and keep track of what is paid, all in one place.

What it does

  • Quotations and invoices with automatic numbering
  • Turns an accepted quote into an invoice in one click
  • Tracks Draft, Sent and Paid, and flags overdue invoices
  • Prints or saves a clean PDF
  • Backs up your data. Nothing leaves your device.

Built with care

  • Money is calculated in whole sen, so totals never show rounding errors
  • Automated tests check the calculations and the backup import
  • Works on phone and desktop, in light and dark mode
